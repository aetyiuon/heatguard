# Maps and datasets

## Browse by city

Relief Map starts in Arlington. Select Dallas, Fort Worth or Arlington, then Full city map or an intersecting District 6/30 portion. Dallas County and Nearby are separate scopes. All mapped entries preserves all city/county/district source records and provides secondary full-district views, including places outside the three cities. Browsing does not change the named heat-check location.

District snapshots use the **119th Congress (2025–2027), Census BAS 2026** vintage. These are not the 2026-election / 120th Congress boundaries. City/district portions use actual polygon intersections in `src/data/city-districts.json`; the runtime filters pins by containment and clips trails. City park outlines remain labeled context. Fort Worth intersects District 6 in this vintage.

Search communities, parks, trails or asset IDs; zoom clusters for individual markers and use the list for complete labels. View place shows source and status. Directions use the named heat-check origin; straight-line distance/time is not verified pedestrian access. Suggest a place starts with blank coordinates and rejects locations outside the selected scope; public sharing is separate opt-in.

## Sources and coverage

### Arlington and Dallas

Arlington combines municipal drinking fountains, pavilions, park footprints and eligible hike/bike or mountain-bike trails with OSM suggestions. Retired/inactive records are excluded; municipal fountain labels retain asset IDs. Pavilions use approximate footprint centers. Dallas uses municipal park/existing-trail geometry and OSM water/shade/library candidates. Planned trail segments are excluded. City limits respect islands/holes; some city-managed park geometry near or beyond the limits remains labeled context.

### Fort Worth

The October 24 official collection includes 119 fountain and 241 shelter records, 317 parks and 255 existing off-street trail features. It uses Census city limits, municipal asset layers and NCTCOG paths. OSM refresh was unavailable, so no additional OSM coverage is claimed. City/district intersections are generated by the same DFW collector.

### Dallas County

The full county polygon (`GEOID=48113`) is distinct from Dallas city and the congressional scopes. The collection includes 36 searchable community portions and Unincorporated / other county areas. County-wide OSM remains baseline **0/4 sections completed**. This is an amenity-data gap, not a missing county outline. Zero pins do not establish that a city has no fountains or shade.

The October 23 collection includes 673 clipped NCTCOG existing off-street path features and 29 Garland pavilion candidates. One pavilion-pad record was excluded because it did not establish a roofed shelter. Pavilion centers are approximate; surface, closures, access and working status remain unknown. Prior city source timestamps are retained. Future work includes official amenity layers for Irving, Mesquite, Richardson and southern county communities.

### TX-06 and TX-30 full sources

TX-06 uses Census `GEOID=4806`, nine county portions and 89 searchable community portions, including rural areas beyond DFW. TX-30 uses `GEOID=4830`, Dallas/Tarrant county portions and 19 community portions. Crossing communities are represented by their district portion, not their whole city. The source JSON contains the exact locality and county counts.

Both use Census geography, USGS PAD-US open-access parks, TPWD boundaries/official hiking trails/shelters and dated city records. District-wide OSM collection is incomplete: TX-06 baseline **0/20 sections**, TX-30 baseline **0/2 sections**. Full refresh attempts failed without replacing the previous usable data. A zero boundaryCoverage value describes unfinished OSM queries, not absence of an official district outline. Rural water/shade collection remains incomplete.

## Collection and refresh

| Task                            | Full collection                        | Explicit limited baseline                            |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Arlington + Dallas              | `pnpm data:refresh`                    | None                                                 |
| Fort Worth + city intersections | `pnpm exec tsx scripts/refresh-dfw.ts` | `pnpm data:refresh:dfw` (script includes --baseline) |
| TX-06                           | `pnpm data:refresh:district`           | `pnpm data:district:baseline`                        |
| TX-30                           | `pnpm data:refresh:tx30`               | `pnpm data:tx30:baseline`                            |
| Dallas County                   | `pnpm data:refresh:dallas-county`      | `pnpm data:dallas-county:baseline`                   |

District/county collectors also accept --no-split for bounded provider diagnostics. Do not run refreshes merely to change presentation. Baseline intentionally skips unavailable full OSM collection and must retain its incomplete label.

Collectors enumerate exact ArcGIS object IDs, fetch complete batches and reject missing/partial responses. Exact-ID batches avoid reapplying the spatial envelope that can drop coarse-index hits. OSM queries are uncapped, sectioned, deduplicated and cached for one day under ignored `work/` paths. Points use polygon containment; parks use intersections; crossing lines are clipped even when endpoints are outside. Required-source failures preserve the prior snapshot; final publication occurs only after validation. On Windows the TPWD-specific pwsh fallback uses normally validated HTTPS, never disabled certificate checks.

## APIs and offline limits

`/relief?city=arlington`, `city=dallas`, `city=fort-worth`, `county=dallas`, `district=tx06` and `district=tx30` serve dated snapshots. Use the /api prefix on Vercel. Unsupported, repeated or conflicting scope parameters are rejected. The app loads bundled scopes on demand, allowing lists/search/geometry without a fresh data lookup. Basemap tiles and an uncached Leaflet CDN script still need network; no offline tile-download rights are assumed. Nearby queries retain bounded search radii and provider availability warnings.

All water flow, shade, hours and access remain unverified. Only explicit potable fountain/tap tags count as water; decorative fountains do not. Parks are context, woodlands potential shade, and libraries public cooling candidates rather than designated open cooling centers. Retrieval dates are not inspections. Local unavailable reports replace suggested records; public sharing remains opt-in. See [Verification](verification.md) for checks and outstanding device acceptance.
